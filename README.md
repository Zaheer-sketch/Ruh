# RUH — Premium Streetwear & E-commerce Platform

**Defined by Feeling.**

RUH is a Bangladesh-based premium streetwear brand that combines fashion, music, emotion and South Asian visual culture.

As the founder, I have worked on developing RUH's brand identity, digital storefront, online shopping experience and e-commerce operations.

🌐 **Live Website:** https://ruhstreetwear.com/

## Project Overview

RUH is more than a clothing brand. Its digital platform supports product discovery, shopping, order management and business operations.

The project brings together e-commerce development, user experience, digital marketing and brand entrepreneurship.

## Key Website Features

**Customer-facing experience**
- Responsive, mobile-focused storefront
- Product catalogue with sizes, pricing and availability
- Product detail pages and shopping cart
- Checkout and order placement
- Delivery information and customer support
- Search-engine-friendly product pages

**E-commerce administration**
- Product and inventory management
- Order processing and status tracking
- Product media and measurement management
- Shipping workflow integration
- Customer review management
- Website analytics and conversion event tracking

## Technical Architecture

The wider RUH platform incorporates:

- Database-backed product and order workflows
- Cloudflare D1 for structured data
- Cloudflare R2 for product media storage
- Administrative interfaces for catalogue and store operations
- Pathao delivery workflow integration
- Meta Pixel and GA4 analytics
- SEO metadata and structured product information

RUH's creative direction focuses on typography, Urdu-inspired artwork, storytelling and emotionally driven clothing collections.

The brand uses a burgundy, cream and near-black colour palette.
